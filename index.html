Cards INR

Unpaid card statements, in rupees.
You pay the issuer yourself.

01

Outstanding

What is left on each unpaid statement, added once.

02

Minimum

Known minimums, summed. A blank one stays “Not provided”.

03

Next due

The earliest unpaid due date. Due soon means 7 days.

04

Statement

You enter the statement date, due date, and total due.

05

Payment

Amount and date, after you pay. It cannot exceed what is left.

06

History

Paid statements stay here and leave the outstanding total.
